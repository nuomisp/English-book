# Starter content

The 100 entries in vocabulary.txt and the reading lessons in StarterContent.kt are original starter material written for this project. Definitions and examples are brief beginner-oriented teaching content, not copied from an exam paper or commercial word book. Pronunciation uses broad British IPA and is a reference for the displayed headword. The word list is a foundation bridge, not a complete official CET-4 vocabulary list. Listening uses the displayed original example sentences; synthetic narration is practice material, not authentic exam audio.

Vocabulary format: word|ipa|Chinese meaning|English example|Chinese example|level. IDs are stable lowercase English headwords. Updating an entry preserves existing progress by ID.
